Cave Expansion

Eight rare places in the rock, for the Village Friends modpack. You break into them strip-mining or stumble on them caving: a working camp of the Delvers' Guild, a Deepwatch garrison holding a cave road, a hermit's mushroom hollow, and the lost works of the Old Delvers.

How rare they are

In the test world the game made every placement cell in a block of {{CELLS}} cells around spawn (about 4,300 blocks square). {{FOUND}} of them grew a delve, about one per {{PER}} × {{PER}} blocks. A delve always touches a cave somewhere and sits mostly in solid rock, so cavers and strip-miners both find them. config/caveexpansion.json sets "rarity": 2.0 means half as many along each side.

{{BARS}}

The people below

The miners, the Deepwatch and the hermit are Village Friends residents with their own day underground (no tavern, no bell, no weather; the garrison keeps a night watch) and their own lines. Ask them for news and they tell you which way the nearest other delve lies and roughly how far. Miners are a new profession whose job site is the Prospecting Table.

Plays well with the pack

  • Needs Village Friends 2.28 or newer.
  • The Plata Clan: delves never generate within 3 chunks of a Scale Den or 5 chunks of a Silver Warren. Miners have heard stories of the silver folk.
  • Also keeps clear of villages, homesteads, trial chambers, ancient cities, strongholds and ocean monuments; never in the Deep Dark.
  • Not-So-Vanilla Mobs: its Lost Miners, Gravewarden and Soulpyres guard the delves when installed.
  • Game test run with: {{MODS}}.
